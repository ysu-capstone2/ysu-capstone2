## 3. 물리 ERD 9개 테이블 및 DB 마이그레이션 구조

[`supabase/migrations/20261023193000_complete_erd_and_processes.sql`](file:///Users/yiinsu/Google%20Drive/My%20Drive/Github/ysu-capstone2/supabase/migrations/20261023193000_complete_erd_and_processes.sql)에 정의된 전체 테이블 구조:

1. **`admin_knowledge` (행정지식베이스)**: `id`, `category_code`, `question_type`, `title`, `content`, `law_clause`, `required_docs`, `fee`, `issuance_place`, `keywords`, `revised_at`, `version_no`, `approval_status`
2. **`chat_session` (민원대화기록)**: `id`, `knowledge_id`, `citizen_id`, `channel`, `query`, `answer`, `confidence_score`, `status`, `issue_summary`, `chunk_ref`, `is_self_resolved`, `transfer_reason`, `created_at`
3. **`citizen_complaints` (상담예약및이관이력 + 민원처리대장 통합)**: `id`, `complaint_no`, `user_id`, `request_id`, `session_id`, `citizen_name`, `citizen_tel`, `dept_code`, `dept_name`, `consult_type`, `desired_datetime`, `transfer_reason`, `summary`, `ai_summary`, `process_status`, `officer_name`, `due_date`, `review_memo`, `investigation_note`, `supplement_request`, `supplement_submission`, `result`, `created_at`, `updated_at`
4. **`department_info` (관할기관및부서정보)**: `dept_code`, `org_code`, `org_name`, `dept_name`, `category_code`, `jurisdiction`, `location`, `tel`
5. **`consult_slot` (상담창구일정슬롯)**: `id`, `org_code`, `dept_code`, `slot_date`, `start_time`, `end_time`, `max_capacity`, `booked_count`, `is_closed`
6. **`service_counters` & `queue_tickets` (창구대기현황 및 번호표 큐)**: 원자적 트랜잭션 RPC (`portal_join_queue`, `portal_call_next`, `portal_cancel_ticket`) 지원
7. **`notification_log` (알림발송이력)**: `id`, `session_id`, `complaint_no`, `recipient_tel`, `notif_type`, `channel`, `message_body`, `is_success`, `created_at`
8. **`complaint_statistics` (민원통계데이터)** 및 **`portal_summary()` RPC**
